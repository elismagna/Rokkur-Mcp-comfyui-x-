Night School

Practice renders one shot of a finished video several ways: as the studio would render it, with only a new seed, and with one setting changed at a time. Each render is scored for general quality, never for anyone's taste: half what QC measured, half the AI picture review, and a render with broken bodies is capped at {{ '%.0f'|format(5) }} however steady it is. A change becomes a lesson only when it beats the baseline by more than a new seed alone does, on {{ settings.practice.min_batches }} or more shots. Your videos are never changed.
